01 — About the congress

A space for questions
that shape the future.

The National Children’s Science Congress encourages children to connect scientific thinking with the world around them—turning everyday observations into meaningful investigations.

01
⌕

Explore real questions

Identify problems in local surroundings and approach them through observation, evidence and inquiry.

02
⌁

Learn through discovery

Build confidence in scientific methods, teamwork, experimentation and communicating findings.

03
✳

Create positive impact

Develop practical ideas that respond to community needs and encourage responsible innovation.

Why it matters

It gives students the opportunity to become active participants in science—not just learners of scientific facts.

02 — The host institute

Rooted in learning.
Driven by innovation.

Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee International Institute of Information Technology, Naya Raipur (IIIT-NR) is a state university established by the Government of Chhattisgarh. The institute focuses on education, research and technological innovation for social upliftment.
